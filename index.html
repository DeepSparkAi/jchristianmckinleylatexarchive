Do Not Vote the Rich Away

Why the Poor Voter Loses by Confiscating the Wealth That Serves Him

ECON 10 · May 2026 · J. Christian McKinley

The paper argues that confiscating productive wealth harms the non-wealthy voter by discouraging the investment, work, and ongoing production on which his own prosperity depends. Its case is made from the voter's self-interest rather than sympathy for wealthy people.
